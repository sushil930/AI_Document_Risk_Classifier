INTERNAL & EXTERNAL GOVERNANCE MEMORANDUM
TO: Evergreen Retail Holdings Corp. Stakeholders & Operations Review Board
FROM: Office of Legal, Risk & Governance, Pinnacle OmniCommerce Platforms, Inc.
DATE: 2024-10-07
SUBJECT: Enterprise Data Processing and Platform Addendum (Ref: DG-CON-2024-0302, Rev 2.3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------


1. PURPOSE, DEFINITIONS, AND SCOPE OF SERVICES
1.1 Scope of Engagement. This Enterprise Data Processing and Platform Addendum ("Agreement") is entered into as of 2024-10-07 by and between Pinnacle OmniCommerce Platforms, Inc., a Georgia corporation with principal offices at 1180 Peachtree Street NE, Suite 1800, Atlanta, GA 30309 ("Provider"), and Evergreen Retail Holdings Corp., a North Carolina entity located at 201 N. Tryon Street, Suite 1300, Charlotte, NC 28202 ("Customer"). Provider shall provision, host, maintain, and support the enterprise software platform, application programming interfaces (APIs), and associated technical environments described in the applicable Order Form (collectively, the "Hosted Services").
1.2 Permitted Users and Affiliates. Customer may authorize its employees, contractors, and designated corporate affiliates to access and use the Hosted Services solely for Customer's internal business operations. Customer remains responsible for ensuring that all Authorized Users comply with the usage parameters and security controls set forth in this Agreement.
1.3 Service Level Commitment and Architecture. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain monthly platform availability of at least 99.9%, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance and force majeure events outside Provider's reasonable control. Provider shall maintain logically isolated tenant environments and redundant database replicas across geographically separated availability zones.

2. CUSTOMER DATA STEWARDSHIP, SUBPROCESSORS, AND COOPERATION
2.1 Ownership of Customer Data. As between the parties, Customer retains all right, title, and interest in and to all electronic data, records, files, and configurations submitted by or on behalf of Customer to the Hosted Services ("Customer Data").
2.2 Subprocessor Governance. Provider shall maintain an up-to-date list of authorized third-party infrastructure and support subprocessors and shall impose written data protection obligations on each subprocessor that are no less protective than those contained in this Agreement.
2.3 Technical Documentation and Reporting. Provider shall make available to Customer standard operational telemetry, quarterly service performance summaries, and annual third-party audit attestations (including SOC 2 Type II executive summaries) upon written request.

3. IRREVOCABLE COMMITMENT, DATA RIGHTS, AND REMEDY WAIVERS
3.1 Binding Multi-Year Commitment. This Agreement takes effect on 2024-10-07 and establishes a binding commercial framework governing all Customer deployments across Provider's infrastructure.
3.2 Controlling Risk Allocation and Obligation Clause. Customer's payment and minimum commitment obligations under this Agreement are irrevocable and non-cancellable for the full sixty (60) month term, and Customer expressly waives any right to suspend performance, withhold fees, or terminate this Agreement even in the event of an uncured material breach, prolonged service outage, or security failure by Provider.
3.3 Unilateral Modification of Service Scope. Provider reserves the unilateral right to modify, deprecate, or discontinue any core platform feature, security module, or API endpoint at any time without prior notice and without any reduction in Customer's committed fees.

4. INDEMNIFICATION, DISCLAIMERS, AND GOVERNING PROVISIONS
4.1 Survival of Customer Obligations. All fee commitments, license grants to Customer Data, and Customer indemnification covenants shall survive any expiration, suspension, or purported termination of this Agreement in perpetuity.
4.2 Injunctive Relief and Venue. Provider may seek immediate ex parte injunctive relief in the state or federal courts of Georgia without posting bond, while Customer must submit any claim exclusively to confidential arbitration after paying all disputed and future contract balances in full.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY, COMPLIANCE, AND MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
5.1 Confidentiality Undertakings. Subject to the express license and data rights in Section 3, each party agrees to apply reasonable administrative and physical safeguards to protect non-public business information disclosed by the other party and marked as confidential.
5.2 Force Majeure and Assignment. Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by natural disasters or governmental acts beyond reasonable control. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Georgia, without regard to conflict of law principles.
5.3 Entire Agreement and Execution. This Agreement, together with its referenced Schedules and Order Forms, constitutes the entire understanding between Pinnacle OmniCommerce Platforms, Inc. and Evergreen Retail Holdings Corp. regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior proposals or communications.
