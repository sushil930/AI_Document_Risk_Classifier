1. FEE STRUCTURE, INVOICING SCHEDULE, AND CURRENCY SETTLEMENT
1.1 Subscription and Usage Fees. This Enterprise Fee Schedule, Payment Terms, and Billing Policy ("Financial Schedule") governs all fees, invoicing procedures, tax responsibilities, audit rights, and financial liability limits between Stratos Sovereign Cloud Services, Inc. ("Billing Entity") and BlueRidge Civic Credit Union ("Payor") effective 2026-12-20.
1.2 Invoicing Cadence and Settlement. Unless otherwise specified in an executed Order Form, recurring base subscription fees are invoiced annually in advance, and metered consumption charges are invoiced monthly in arrears based on verified telemetry logs. All undisputed invoice amounts are due and payable in United States Dollars (USD) via ACH or wire transfer within 45 calendar days of Payor's receipt of a valid itemized invoice.
1.3 Tax Documentation. Fees are exclusive of applicable sales, use, value-added, or withholding taxes. Billing Entity shall provide a valid IRS Form W-9 (or W-8BEN-E where applicable) and itemize statutory taxes separately on each invoice.

2. GOOD-FAITH BILLING DISPUTES AND METERED USAGE VERIFICATION
2.1 Invoice Dispute Procedure. Payor may withhold payment of any line item disputed reasonably and in good faith by providing written notice and supporting documentation to Billing Entity prior to the invoice due date, while timely remitting all undisputed portions of the invoice.
2.2 Usage Reconciliation. Billing Entity shall provide Payor with daily access to an itemized usage dashboard displaying seat counts, API call volumes, and storage consumption metrics used to calculate metered charges.

3. TRUE-UP AUDITS, RATE ADJUSTMENTS, AND REFUND LIMITATIONS
3.1 Specific Financial Condition. Payor must submit any claim for billing errors, duplicate charges, or over-invoicing in writing within twenty (20) calendar days of the invoice date, after which the invoice shall be deemed final, conclusive, and permanently waived by Payor regardless of subsequent audit findings.
3.2 Standard Liability Ceiling. Subject to the fee, audit, and surcharge obligations in Section 3.1, each party's aggregate liability for direct damages under this Financial Schedule shall not exceed the total fees paid by Payor during the preceding 24 months.
3.3 Collection and Suspension Notice. If an undisputed balance remains unpaid for more than fifteen (15) days after written past-due notice, Billing Entity may suspend non-production sandbox environments until payment is received.

4. FINANCIAL REPORTING AND RECORDKEEPING
4.1 Record Retention. Both parties shall maintain accurate accounting and usage records for at least three (3) years following the close of each fiscal year.
4.2 Currency Conversion. Cross-border settlements converted into USD shall utilize the spot exchange rate published by the Federal Reserve on the invoice issuance date.

5. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS AND TREASURY CONTACTS
5.1 Remittance Instructions. All wire and ACH settlements shall reference Document Code DG-FIN-2026-0256 and be directed to the treasury account designated by Stratos Sovereign Cloud Services, Inc., 1120 NW Couch Street, Suite 700, Portland, OR 97209.
5.2 Severability and Integration. This Financial Schedule forms an integral part of the commercial relationship between Stratos Sovereign Cloud Services, Inc. and BlueRidge Civic Credit Union as of 2026-12-20.
