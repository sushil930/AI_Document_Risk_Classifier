STANDARD OPERATING & COMPLIANCE SPECIFICATION
Title: Cloud Telemetry, Subprocessor, and Cross-Border Privacy Notice
Control Identifier: DG-PRI-2024-0314 (v2.6)
Publication Date: 2024-10-13
Custodian Organization: Pinnacle OmniCommerce Platforms, Inc. (Applicable to Redwood Regional Medical Center)
================================================================================


1. PURPOSE, DATA CLASSIFICATION, AND GOVERNANCE SCOPE
1.1 Policy Objective. This Cloud Telemetry, Subprocessor, and Cross-Border Privacy Notice ("Policy") establishes the technical, administrative, and organizational controls maintained by Pinnacle OmniCommerce Platforms, Inc. ("Organization") across all production services provided to Redwood Regional Medical Center and affiliated enterprise tenants as of 2024-10-13.
1.2 Data Classification Tiers. Organization classifies managed information into four sensitivity tiers: (a) Public Reference Data; (b) Internal Operational Telemetry; (c) Confidential Customer Business Records; and (d) Restricted Personal and Regulated Data, including personally identifiable information (PII), authentication credentials, and financial account identifiers.
1.3 Architecture and Tenant Isolation. Production workloads execute within virtual private cloud environments utilizing hardware-assisted virtualization, network micro-segmentation, and dedicated customer key hierarchies.

2. CRYPTOGRAPHIC STANDARDS, ACCESS LOGGING, AND VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT
2.1 Encryption in Transit and at Rest. All external and service-to-service network traffic is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3), and persistent storage volumes are encrypted at rest using AES-256 block ciphers managed through a FIPS 140-3 validated key management service.
2.2 Vulnerability Scanning and Penetration Testing. Organization conducts automated container and dependency vulnerability scans daily, remediates critical CVEs within seven (7) days of disclosure, and engages an independent CREST-accredited security firm to perform annual application and infrastructure penetration tests.

3. UNRESTRICTED DATA ACCESS, RETENTION, AND DISCLOSURE PROVISIONS
3.1 Primary Data Access and Sharing Directive. To facilitate rapid debugging and cross-regional analytics, Organization replicates unencrypted production database snapshots—including raw personally identifiable information, plaintext session tokens, and financial records—to shared development clusters accessible to all employees and offshore third-party contractors without multi-factor authentication or individual audit logging.
3.2 Absence of Technical Segregation. Organization may co-mingle Customer Data with datasets from other commercial partners in shared storage buckets and does not guarantee logical or cryptographic separation between production, staging, and partner analytics environments.
3.3 Waiver of Security Audits. Customer may not conduct security assessments, request penetration test results, or inspect Organization's logging infrastructure under any circumstances.

4. POLICY MODIFICATION AND RISK ASSUMPTION
4.1 Unilateral Amendment. Organization may modify its data handling and security practices at any time in its sole discretion without notice to Customer.
4.2 Assumption of Security Risk. Customer assumes full responsibility for all regulatory inquiries, data subject claims, and operational losses resulting from the data handling practices described in this Policy.

5. DEFINITIONS, COMPLIANCE CONTACTS, AND DOCUMENT CONTROL
5.1 Regulatory Definitions. Terms such as 'Personal Data,' 'Controller,' 'Processor,' and 'Security Incident' shall be interpreted in light of the operational provisions set forth in Sections 1 through 4 of this Policy.
5.2 Document Custodian. Inquiries regarding this Policy should be directed to the Office of Information Governance at Pinnacle OmniCommerce Platforms, Inc., 1180 Peachtree Street NE, Suite 1800, Atlanta, GA 30309 (Reference Code: DG-PRI-2024-0314).
